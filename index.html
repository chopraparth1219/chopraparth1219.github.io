Hackathon timeline

    Smart India Hackathon presentation

    Problem

    Design and develop a smart, solar-powered drying and compact packaging system to support home-based agarbatti manufacturing by rural women artisans.

    Solution

    A solar-hybrid powered drying and packaging unit for home-based agarbatti manufacturing. It replaces inconsistent open-air sun drying with a controlled, automated, hygienic process.

    Key design decisions

    • Enclosed UV-C chamber that shuts off automatically when opened, following standard UV-C safety practices
    • Low-cost, readily available battery types in a modular, replaceable design
    • Field calibration and testing across multiple regions and climates before final deployment
    • Voice-first, icon-based companion app that needs minimal text input
